Game Live Subtitles - User Guide
================================
中文說明請看「使用說明.txt」

What it does
------------
Turns what other people say in Discord or in a game into live subtitles that
float over your screen, showing the original speech plus a translation.
It can translate English → Chinese, or Chinese → English.


Getting started
---------------
1. Extract the whole zip file (don't take the exe out on its own; the
   _internal folder next to it must stay there)
2. Double-click "遊戲即時字幕.exe" (the program's Chinese name, meaning
   "Game Live Subtitles")
3. If the window shows Chinese, pick "English" in the language menu at the
   top right
4. Follow "How to use" in the window, then press "Start"


Translation direction
---------------------
Under "Translation", pick the language the other person speaks:
- They speak English → "English → Chinese": English on top, Chinese below
- They speak Chinese → "Chinese → English": Chinese on top, English below
Picking the wrong one gives no text, or gibberish.


Language
--------
Switch between "繁體中文" (Traditional Chinese) and "English" in the menu at
the top right of the window. The log and the hints on the subtitles switch too.
On first launch it follows your Windows language: Chinese Windows shows
Chinese, any other language shows English.


Translate only one app (e.g. only Discord)
------------------------------------------
Pick Discord.exe in "Only this app". In-game characters speaking English
won't be translated; only real people talking in Discord become subtitles.

Not in the list? Make that app play a sound once (e.g. ask someone to say
something), then press "Refresh" next to it. Once an app is picked,
"Audio source" is no longer used and is greyed out.


First run
---------
- The first time you press "Start", it may take 1–2 minutes to respond.
  Windows antivirus is scanning the files; it is not frozen. After that it
  opens in about 3 seconds.
- The "Standard" speech recognition model is already included, so there is
  nothing to download before you can start.
- Windows may show "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info" → "Run anyway".
  (This appears because the program isn't signed with a paid digital
  certificate. It is not a virus.)


Graphics card
-------------
- With an NVIDIA graphics card, it uses the GPU automatically and is much faster.
- Without one, it uses the CPU instead. It still works, but subtitles lag
  1–2 seconds; set "Accuracy" to "Fast" or "Standard", not "Accurate".


Translator
----------
- Google Translate is the default and needs no setup.
- For more natural, conversational translations you can pick Gemini, but it
  is slower and needs a free API key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey


Moving and resizing the subtitles
---------------------------------
- Drag the subtitle bar anywhere with the mouse.
- Drag the "◢" grip at the bottom-right to change its width.
- Hotkeys that also work in game:
    Ctrl+Alt+Arrow keys → move the subtitles
    Ctrl+Alt+L          → lock / unlock (locked = clicks pass through, so
                          it won't block your game)
- Position and width are remembered for next time.


Changing the accuracy (Fast / Accurate)
---------------------------------------
Only "Standard" is included. If you switch "Accuracy" to "Fast" or
"Accurate", the line below says it hasn't been downloaded yet: press
"Download" on the right and wait for the progress bar.
- "Fast" is about 140 MB, "Accurate" about 1.5 GB. On a slow connection
  "Accurate" can take half an hour or more.
- You can press "Cancel" while downloading. What was already downloaded is
  kept, so it resumes from there next time.
- Each model only has to be downloaded once.
- If you press "Start" before downloading, it asks whether to download now.


Troubleshooting
---------------
- Can't see the subtitles → set the game to "Borderless window";
  exclusive fullscreen hides them.
- Subtitles keep popping up or show strange words → raise "Volume threshold".
- Nothing happens when people talk → in "Audio source", pick the speakers or
  headphones that play the sound, or lower "Volume threshold".
- No text or gibberish → check that "Translation" matches the language being
  spoken.
- Can't close the subtitles after turning on click-through → go back to the
  launcher window and press "Stop".
- The translation line keeps showing "—" → Google Translate may be
  temporarily unreachable; try Gemini instead.
- The model download keeps failing → check your internet connection and press
  "Download" again. "Standard" needs no download at all, so you can switch
  back to it in the meantime.
